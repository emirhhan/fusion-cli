## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: simple abstract mascot for Fusion AI productivity app. Create ONE very simple original non-animal character, in the visual simplicity of a language-learning app mascot or minimalist AI Dots companion, but do not copy any existing character. A single compact softly rounded blue squircle/blob body, two small flat dark oval eyes and one tiny neutral friendly mouth. Two very short simple rounded arms and two tiny stub feet integrated into its body. Restrained adult-friendly charm. Flat simple shapes with only very subtle soft 3D volume, smooth matte finish; no realism, no fur, no plush texture, no glossy big eyes. ABSOLUTELY NOT an animal: no ears, horns, snout, beak, muzzle, wings, fins, tail, feathers or animal anatomy. No robot armor, panels, accessories, headphones, clothes, belly patch or complex details. Simple cyan-blue main fill and a slight violet-blue shaded edge only. Entire design must be legible as a tiny app icon. Front-facing full body centered, square canvas, 15% clear margin. Actual transparent alpha background, no floor, cast shadow, checkerboard, scene, text, watermark or logo. The key requirement is simplicity and abstraction, NOT a detailed creature.
